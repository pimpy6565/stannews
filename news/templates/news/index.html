The Great Pepsi Pennsauken Bubble Mystery

By Stan Reporter • Pennsauken, NJ

PENNSAUKEN, NJ — Workers at the Pepsi-Cola & National Brand Beverages plant on North Crescent Boulevard thought it was just another loud Tuesday on the bottling line.

Then the bubbles started acting weird.

At around 10:45 a.m., every single can of Pepsi rolling off the line began producing massive, perfectly round bubbles that refused to pop. Not foam. Not fizz. These were shiny, basketball-sized bubbles that floated gently toward the ceiling like they were filled with helium instead of carbonation.

Line supervisor Maria Gonzalez grabbed her phone and started filming as one bubble lifted a half-full case of Mountain Dew three feet in the air before gently setting it back down.

“It looked like the soda was throwing a party and we weren’t invited,” she told Stan News.

By lunchtime, the entire warehouse was filled with floating Pepsi bubbles. Delivery drivers walked in, got trapped in a bubble cluster, and emerged laughing uncontrollably with giant smiles on their faces.

One veteran truck driver, who’s been hauling Pepsi out of Pennsauken for 22 years, said: “I’ve seen flat soda, I’ve seen exploding soda, but I’ve never seen soda that wants to fly.”

Local Theories Went Wild

  • A kid from nearby Camden swore it was “the ghost of the old Coca-Cola plant getting revenge.”
  • A mechanic blamed it on “too much Jersey humidity mixing with the secret Pepsi formula.”
  • One conspiracy enthusiast claimed Pepsi had finally perfected “anti-gravity cola.”

By 4 p.m. the bubbles finally started popping — and when they did, they released the strongest, freshest Pepsi scent the workers had ever smelled. Several employees said it was better than the first sip on a hot summer day.

Stan News Tip: If you spot a stray Pepsi bubble floating over Pennsauken or Camden, snap a picture and send it our way. We’re collecting evidence for the official “Bubble Files.”

Next time you crack open a cold Pepsi from the Pennsauken plant… take a second to listen. You might just hear it whispering, “Want to float today?”
